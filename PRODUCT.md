# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows users with supported Razer mice and keyboards who want to make quick, confident hardware adjustments without running a heavy, service-based gaming utility. They open opsrzr to inspect a connected device, adjust lighting, sensitivity, polling rate, battery, or profiles, then return to their work or game.

## Product Purpose

opsrzr is a lightweight, tray-first Razer Synapse alternative that talks directly to supported hardware over USB HID. Its desktop interface should make device configuration feel dependable and immediate, while staying out of the way.

## Brand Personality

Quietly luxurious, composed, and precise. It should feel like a premium hardware companion: calm enough for everyday use, tactile without theatrics, and clearly independent from Razer's gaming-software visual language.

## Anti-references

- Razer Synapse's RGB-heavy, crowded gaming-utility aesthetic.
- Generic AI/SaaS dashboard patterns: oversized metric cards, gradient decoration, repeated pills, or “glow” used as hierarchy.
- A phone interface merely stretched into a desktop window.
- Harsh pure-black surfaces, low-contrast gray text, and color-only status communication.

## Design Principles

1. Make the device the subject; the interface is the companion.
2. Earn every color: use it for status, selection, and real actions—not atmosphere.
3. Preserve desktop efficiency with a stable device rail and a wide, scannable configuration canvas.
4. Use calm materials, soft contrast, and clear typography to reduce visual fatigue.
5. Prefer grouped settings and progressive detail over a field of floating cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast requirements. Support `prefers-reduced-motion`, provide a high-contrast theme or mode, and never rely on color alone for device, battery, or error status. Maintain visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, accessible names for icon controls, and comfortable hit targets.
